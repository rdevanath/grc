    RBI IT Governance Directions, 2023

    The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) issued a comprehensive regulation titled “Information Technology Governance, Risk, Controls and Assurance Practices Directions, 2023”, These directions apply to: Scheduled commercial banks, NBFCs, payment banks, credit bureaus, Financial Institutions.

    Structured breakdown of the key mandates:

    IT Governance Framework

    • Board Oversight
      Establish an IT Strategy Committee and ensure IT governance is aligned with business goals.
    • Senior Management Role
      Must implement and monitor IT policies and controls.
    • IT Steering Committee
      Required to oversee IT projects and performance.

    IT Infrastructure & Services Management

    • Third-Party Arrangements
      Must be governed by robust contracts and risk assessments.
    • Change & Patch Management
      Formalized procedures for updates and fixes.
    • Audit Trails & Logging
      Mandatory for all critical applications to ensure traceability.
    • Cryptographic Controls
      Strong encryption standards for data transmission and storage.

    IT & Information Security Risk Management

    • Cybersecurity Policy
      Must be documented and reviewed annually.
    • Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing
      Required periodically.
    • Incident Response
      Entities must analyze cyber incidents and implement corrective/preventive actions.

    Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery

    • BCP & DR Policy
      Must be in place and tested regularly.
    • Operational Resilience
      IT systems should support uninterrupted services during disruptions.

    Information Systems Audit

    • IS Audit
      Must be conducted independently and cover all critical systems.
    • Audit Frequency
      Should align with risk profile and regulatory expectations.

    Other key Financial regulations

    SEBI Cybersecurity & Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF)

    • The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) regulates key market intermediaries
    • Applies to: Stock exchanges, Stock brokers, Asset Management Companies (AMC), KYC Registration Agencies (KRA), Credit Rating Agencies (CRA), Alternative Investment Funds (AIF)
    • Key Provisions: Create a single, risk-based cybersecurity and resilience baseline across the securities market, strengthening governance, incident response, third-party oversight, and continuous monitoring (SOC).
    • Penalties: SEBI directions, suspension, fines under SEBI Act

    IRDAI Cybersecurity Guidelines, 2023

    • Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) regulates insurance sector
    • Applies to: Insurers and a broad ecosystem of intermediaries (e.g., brokers, web aggregators, repositories, surveyors, information bureau, Insurance Self Network Platform)
    • Key Provisions: Mandate an enterprise-wide ISMS with board oversight, documented cyber policies, CISO appointment, periodic risk assessments, incident response, and continuous assurance to reduce fraud and protect policyholder data.
    • Penalties: IT Act-linked liabilities and IRDAI enforcement actions
